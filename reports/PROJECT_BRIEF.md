# Project brief

## Decision to support
Choose where a retention team should focus first: protect active high-value customers, encourage promising customers to return, or run a low-cost win-back test for valuable customers who have gone quiet.

## Analytical approach
Customer-level RFM, customer value concentration, calendar-month cohort retention, first-to-second purchase timing and a 90-day repeat-purchase ranking. A scenario planner makes the contact cost and contribution assumptions visible.

## Deliverables
A standalone story dashboard, a short business report, a SQLite database, SQL queries, static charts, a customer-level output table and a documented reproducible pipeline.

## Decision standard
An insight is only useful when the data can support it. The source permits analysis of observed purchase value and repeat behaviour. It does not support true profit calculations or a complete predictive lifetime-value estimate without extra cost, margin and future-observation data.
