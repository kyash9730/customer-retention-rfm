# Charts in this folder

The charts are regenerated by `python run_project.py --source demo` or `python run_project.py --source real --download`.

- `segment_value_mix.png` - customer share compared with share of historic purchase value.
- `customer_value_concentration.png` - cumulative share of customer value by customer rank.
- `cohort_retention_heatmap.png` - calendar-month retention by first-purchase cohort.
- `second_purchase_timing.png` - time from first to second invoice among repeat buyers.
- `retention_value_map.png` - recency against historic value; bubble size shows purchase frequency.
- `repeat_model_decile_lift.png` - observed repeat rate by prediction score decile in the latest hold-back window.

When demo mode is active, the charts are labelled synthetic. They must not be cited as real business findings.
